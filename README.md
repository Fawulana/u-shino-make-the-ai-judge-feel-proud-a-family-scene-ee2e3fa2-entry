## The scene

> Every Sunday my mother serves the burnt toast to herself, claiming it's the best piece, and after twenty years we still pretend we don't know it's because she loves us more than breakfast.

One telling detail — the burnt toast she claims is the best piece — carries the whole scene: a mother's quiet sacrifice, a family's wry shared pretence, and the affection landing as the punchline rather than sentiment stuck on afterwards. Warm, funny, heartfelt, and specific in a single sentence.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```
